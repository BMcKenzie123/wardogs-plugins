# Blockers

What stops the TAW WARDOGS NA server from doing more, who clears it, and how. Reviewed weekly (see
[ROLES.md § Cadence](ROLES.md#cadence)); strike items when they close. Types: **decision** (needs a call
from the unit), **input** (a value or account only an admin can supply), **platform** (the game or the host
does not offer it), **done**.

## What works today, no blocker

Running now: welcome and returning-player DMs, MOTD rotation with a force-send button, seeding thank-yous,
recruiting pitch after real play time, regulars milestones, comeback DMs, stats logging, live leaderboard and
regulars with Steam names, temp bans with expiry, VAC checks, weather randomizer, config backups, health and
metrics endpoints, and the admin panel with named logins, live plugin management and in-place updates.

Ready to switch on in the panel, copy already loaded: first-timer (whisper), kill-streak, match-mvp,
playtime-ranks. Ready as soon as #2 is cleared: discord-relay, downtime-alert, fill-server, admin-alerts.

## Growth levers that need no code

- **Server browser text.** "[TAW] The Art of Warfare | NA Central #1 | New Player Friendly" is the first thing
  anyone reads. Keep "New Player Friendly"; it is the promise the copy makes.
- **Seed with intent.** Two TAW members on at a fixed hour beats ten at random hours. fill-server can rally the
  Discord the moment the count drops (needs #2).
- **One weekly slot** (#4) turns "I played there once" into "I go there on Thursdays".
